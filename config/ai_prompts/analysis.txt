## Your Analysis Task

Analyze this vulnerability finding and provide:

1. **Exploitability Assessment**
   - Can this vulnerability be exploited in the current context?
   - What prerequisites are needed?
   - What is the attack complexity?

2. **Business Impact Analysis**
   - What data or systems could be compromised?
   - What is the potential business damage?
   - Could this lead to compliance violations (GDPR, PCI-DSS, etc.)?

3. **Attack Scenario**
   - Describe a realistic attack scenario step-by-step
   - What would an attacker need to do?
   - How could this be chained with other vulnerabilities?

4. **Confidence Level**
   - How confident are you in this assessment? (0.0-1.0)
   - What additional information would increase confidence?

5. **Recommended Actions**
   - Immediate mitigation steps
   - Long-term remediation
   - Detection mechanisms
