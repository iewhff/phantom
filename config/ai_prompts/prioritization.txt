## Prioritization Criteria

Consider these factors when prioritizing:

1. **Exploitability (40%)**
   - Is there a public exploit available?
   - What's the attack complexity?
   - Does it require authentication?

2. **Business Impact (30%)**
   - What data/systems are at risk?
   - What's the potential financial impact?
   - Are there compliance implications?

3. **Exposure (20%)**
   - Is this internet-facing?
   - How many users/systems are affected?
   - Is sensitive data involved?

4. **Effort to Fix (10%)**
   - Quick wins vs complex changes
   - Dependencies on other fixes
   - Testing requirements
