## Known Exploit Chain Patterns

Look for these common chains:
1. **Information Disclosure → Authentication Bypass → Privilege Escalation**
2. **XSS → CSRF → Account Takeover**
3. **SQLi → Data Exfiltration → Lateral Movement**
4. **SSRF → Internal Service Access → RCE**
5. **File Upload → Path Traversal → RCE**
6. **XXE → SSRF → Internal Network Access**
7. **Broken Access Control → Data Theft → Business Impact**

## Analysis Instructions

1. Identify vulnerabilities that could be chained together
2. Consider the order of exploitation
3. Assess the combined impact (often greater than sum of parts)
4. Provide step-by-step attack path
5. Estimate likelihood of successful exploitation
